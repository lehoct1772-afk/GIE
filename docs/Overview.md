# Geometric Intelligence Engine (GIE)

## Purpose

The Geometric Intelligence Engine (GIE) is an open research project focused on discovering, modeling, and visualizing the mathematical structure underlying geometric phenomena.

The project combines geometry, mathematics, computation, visualization, and artificial intelligence to build a framework capable of identifying geometric relationships across natural and engineered systems.

## Objectives

- Build a reusable geometric analysis framework.
- Develop mathematical models of geometric structures.
- Create visualization tools.
- Document research and discoveries.
- Produce open-source software and documentation.
